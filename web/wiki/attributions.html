The Vitruvian Man

Fri created The Vitruvian Man, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

DynLex centers and uniformly scales the model, converts its surface and shallow interior into a deterministic density point cloud, spatially pairs neighboring regions with a procedural motorcycle point cloud, and expands the shared points into micro-triangles for live WebGL rendering. The original model geometry is not included in the repository.
