The Vitruvian Man

Fri created The Vitruvian Man, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

DynLex centers and uniformly scales the model, converts its surface and shallow interior into a deterministic density point cloud, spatially pairs neighboring regions with a procedural motorcycle point cloud, and expands the shared points into micro-triangles for live WebGL rendering. The original model geometry is not included in the repository.

River Challenge Sound Effects

The ambient forest and river bed is a compact, circularly crossfaded edit of Garuda1982's Forest with small river birds and nature field recording, used under the Pixabay Content License. The 3:41 source recording was reduced to a 30-second loop for the challenge.

The boat uses DRAGON-STUDIO's Simple Whoosh; the sheep's idle call is Universfield's Sheep Bleat; and the win cue is Tithuh's Level up! 03. These sounds are used under the Pixabay Content License.

The anxious call is the Pixabay distribution of EduFigueres's Baby lamb calling his mother, distributed under CC0 1.0. The challenge trims the call and raises its pitch by three semitones during playback.

The rowing cue is the first stroke from Joseph Sardin's Rowing slowly recording, also distributed under CC0 1.0. The source recordings were trimmed and faded to match their animations.
